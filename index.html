📏 骨度分寸法 (Proportional Bone Measurement)

WHO #2998

定義：以體表骨節為主要標誌，測量周身各部的長度和寬度，並按一定比例等分為若干分寸作為取穴的標準，不論男女老幼、高矮胖瘦均適用。

"A method to use landmarks on the body surface, primarily joints, to measure the length and width of various parts of the body. Then the width or length is divided respectively into definite numbers of equal units as the standards for the proportional measurement."

✋ 手指同身寸法 (Finger-Cun Measurement · WHO #2999)

以患者本人的手指寬度或長度作為量取腧穴的標準。

1. 拇指同身寸 (Thumb Measurement · WHO #3000) = 1 寸

以患者拇指指間關節的寬度作為 1 寸。

Width of the interphalangeal joint of the patient's thumb = 1 cun.

2. 中指同身寸 (Middle Finger Measurement · WHO #3001) = 1 寸

以患者中指屈曲時，中節內側兩端紋頭之間的距離作為 1 寸。

Distance between the two radial creases of the flexed middle finger = 1 cun.

3. 橫指同身寸 / 一夫法 (Four-Finger Measurement · WHO #3002) = 3 寸

將食、中、無名、小指併攏，以中指近側指間關節橫紋水平線處四指寬度作為 3 寸。

Width of the 4 fingers close together at the level of the proximal IP joint of the middle finger = 3 cun.
